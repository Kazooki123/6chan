# Welcome to @6chan

Heyya!

## What is this place?

@6chan is a modern imageboard built from scratch. No legacy cruft,
no algorithmic feeds, no accounts required. Just boards, threads,
and honest conversation.

We have:

- Multiple boards for different topics
- Full markdown support in posts
- Audio posting with cover art and lyrics sync
- A real search engine with fuzzy matching
- Reactions without mob-vote counts
- Polls you can attach to any post

## The philosophy

Read the [Freedom of Speech](/etc/fos.html) and
[Privacy Policy](/etc/privacy.html) pages if you want to understand
how we approach moderation and data.

Short version: we moderate behaviour, not belief. Your data is hashed
or never collected in the first place.

## What's coming

- Tripcodes (backend written in Go — coming soon)
- Thread BGM designation
- More boards as the community grows

See you in the threads.
