Global Rules

Read before posting. Ignorance is not an excuse.

  1. Do not post, request, or link to ANY content that is illegal in your jurisdiction.
  2. No spam, flooding, or repeated low-effort posts.
  3. Stay on topic for the board you are posting in.
  4. No doxxing - do not share personal information of others without consent.
  5. Treat other users with basic respect. Targeted harassment is not allowed.
  6. NSFW content must be clearly marked and is only permitted on designated boards.
  7. Do not impersonate moderators, administrators, or other users.
  8. The moderation team has final say. Decisions may be appealed politely via the meta board.
  9. No AI-slop content unless under specific reasons (e.g satirical humor and other context(s)).

Board-Specific Rules

Each board may have additional rules posted at the top of that board. Always read them.

Consequences

Violations may result in post deletion, a temporary ban, or a permanent ban depending on severity. Ban evasion results in an immediate permanent ban.
