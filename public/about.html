About 6chan

Who are We?

We are an independent group of developers, contributors, maintainers, and community members building 6chan as an Open Source experiment in what an anonymous-first community looks like OR can be. 6chan was created NOT by a large company or organization, but by people WHO enjoy building software, exploring the internet, and preserving the freedom and creativity of older online communities while experimenting with modern technology. We come from different backgrounds and interests, but share the same goal: to make 6chan openly, responsibly, and collaboratively, while keeping anonymity, privacy, free expression, and community at the heart of the project!

What is 6chan?

6chan is an anonymous imageboard built around open discussions, community, experimentation, and user privacy.

Unlike conventional social media platforms, 6chan is not designed around follower counts, personal branding, engangement bait bullshit, or even algorithmically "optimized" feeds. Its primary focus is conversation itself! Users create threads, share ideas, discuss subjects across different communities, and participate without being required to build a public identity.

6chan is usually a social platform in abroad sense, but its structure is intentionally different from modern social networking websites and apps. Anonymity and pseudonymity are core parts of its design rather than optional settings 🩷

Why does it exist?

The internet was built around communication between people, but many modern platforms increasingly resolve it around engagements, personal identity, AI-based recommendation systems, and commercial incentives..

6chan is an experiment in doing things differently.

We (as a community) want to create a place where people can speak, disagree, joke, create, and explore ideas without needing followers, real identities, or a carefully constructed online persona.

Although, that doesn't mean 6chan is a place without rules. Freedom of expression comes with responsibilities, and anonymity does NOT mean that harmful behavior is automatically acceptable. Read our Freedom of Speech page for more information about this topic/subject.

Open Source

6chan is developed as an open source project.

The project uses established open source technologies instead of attempting to reinvent the wheel of modern web stacks. Its infrastructure can be seen here.

We believe that building software also means learning from the software ecosystem around us.

Contributors are very very much welcome to check the source code, experiment with the project, propose improvements, report a problem/bug, and creating a fork.
