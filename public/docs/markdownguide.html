What's not allowed

Raw HTML is stripped - you can't inject <script>, <iframe>, or inline styles.
External images are blocked to prevent tracking pixels.
Links must use http:// or https:// - javascript: and data: URIs are stripped.
Deeply nested lists beyond 10 levels are cut off.
