Freedom of Speech ⚖️

What it means on @6chan, and where the lines are.

You've heard of it, let's talk about it.

Last updated: May 2026
@6chan believes in open discussion. We are not here to be your ideological filter. We don't remove posts because they're uncomfortable, controversial, politically inconvenient, or offensive to a particular group. We do remove content that causes real, concrete harm — not hurt feelings.

This page explains exactly where that line sits, and why.

What freedom of speech means here

Freedom of speech on @6chan means you can discuss nearly anything — politics across the full spectrum, religion, philosophy, culture, taboo topics, uncomfortable history, and opinions that most people would find objectionable. That is the point.

We don't operate a safe space. We don't shadow-ban. We don't algorithmically suppress viewpoints. If a post follows the rules, it stays up regardless of whether the staff agrees with it.

Moderation on @6chan is about behaviour, not belief. What you think is your business. How you express it is governed by rules.

What you can discuss freely

  • Political opinions across the full spectrum — left-wing, right-wing, and everything else
  • Religious belief, criticism of religion, and atheism
  • Controversial historical events and their interpretations
  • Cultural criticism, including of majority and minority groups equally
  • Satire and dark humor — labelled clearly when context matters
  • Criticism of governments, corporations, public figures, and institutions
  • Uncomfortable scientific or social research, with source citation
  • Personal experiences with sensitive topics — mental health, trauma, identity

These are not exhaustive. When in doubt, post. If it crosses a hard line (see below) it will be removed. If it doesn't, it won't.

The hard limits — where speech causes real harm

These are not political or cultural judgements. They are categories of content where the speech itself constitutes or directly enables harm, regardless of intent.

Hard removed, no appeal:
— Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) or any sexualisation of minors
— Credible threats of violence against specific real people
— Doxxing — posting private personal information of real individuals
— Content illegal in the jurisdiction where @6chan is hosted
— Coordinated harassment campaigns targeting specific users or individuals
Everything outside this list is subject to board-level rules, not site-wide removal. A post being offensive, factually wrong, or widely hated is not grounds for removal.

Automod and human review

@6chan runs an automated moderation layer that flags posts matching certain patterns for human review. Automod does not automatically delete posts — it queues them. A human moderator makes the final call.

False positives happen. If your post was removed and you believe it shouldn't have been, contact us. We review appeals individually.

Anonymity and speech

@6chan is anonymous by default. We believe anonymity is essential to genuine free expression — people speak more honestly when they aren't performing for a follower count or protecting a reputation.

Anonymity does not mean zero accountability. IP addresses are stored privately and hashed for moderation purposes. They are never shared publicly and never sold. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

OVERALL

The answer to speech you disagree with is MORE SPEECH, NOT SILENCE. @6chan is a place to argue, question, challenge, and occasionally be wrong in public. We are not the arbiters of truth and we don't want to be. What we want is a community that can handle hard conversations without turning them into personal attacks — and when it can't, moderation exists to draw that specific line, nothing wider.
