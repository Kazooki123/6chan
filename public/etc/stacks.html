Stacks of 6chan

Core stacks that 6chan uses in development and production.

(Note that the stack list will change.)

PHP
Literally where this imageboard sits at.
Postgres
The main database of the imageboard. Handles images, videos, audios and their metadata, threads & posts, boards, automoderation rules, etc.
MinIO
Usually for storage buckets, later on this might change depending on what storage bucket service is chosen.
Podman
For containerization, a better alternative of Docker as well.
