Privacy Policy

What @6chan collects, stores, and never touches.

Last updated: May 2026
@6chan is anonymous by default and by design. We collect the minimum data necessary to run the site safely. We do not sell data, run ads, build profiles, or share information with third parties except where required by law. This document explains exactly what exists and why.

What we collect and why

Data Stored as Why Retention
IP address (posts) SHA-256 hash + salt Ban enforcement, spam prevention With the post
IP address (reports) SHA-256 hash + salt Prevent report spam 30 days
IP address (bans) Raw IP Ban matching requires exact IP Until ban expires
IP address (reactions) SHA-256 hash + salt One reaction per user per post With the reaction
Post content Plaintext This is the site Until deleted or pruned
Uploaded files On disk This is the site Until thread pruned
Author name Plaintext Display only - defaults to Anon With the post
Settings (theme, font) localStorage only Browser-side preferences Never leaves your device
Raw IPs stored for bans are the only plaintext IPs on the system. Everything else is hashed with a secret salt - even if the database were compromised, individual IPs could not be recovered.

What we never collect

  • Email addresses - there are no accounts and no registration
  • Real names - the name field is optional and anonymous by default
  • Browser fingerprints - we don't run fingerprinting scripts
  • Tracking cookies - no persistent cookies beyond your session
  • Location data - beyond what your IP naturally implies
  • Device identifiers - we have no interest in who your device is
  • Behavioural analytics - no tracking pixels, no third-party analytics
🩷 @6chan is AD-FREE. No advertisers, no tracking, no revenue model that depends on knowing who you are.

Cookies and sessions

@6chan uses a single session cookie to maintain your captcha state during posting. This cookie is session-scoped - it expires when you close your browser and contains no personally identifying information, only a random session ID.

No persistent tracking cookies are set. Your theme and font preferences are stored entirely in your browser's localStorage and never transmitted to our servers.

Uploaded content

Images, videos, and audio files you upload are stored on our servers and served publicly as part of the thread. Do not upload files containing personal metadata you want kept private - EXIF data in images and ID3 tags in audio files may be readable by other users.

We strip no metadata automatically. This is intentional - audio metadata (artist, title, lyrics) is part of the listening experience on @6chan. If you are concerned about EXIF data in photos, strip it before uploading using a tool like ExifTool or your phone's built-in editor.

Files are deleted when their thread is pruned from the board. There is no guarantee of persistence - @6chan is a discussion platform, not an image host.

Moderation and data access

Moderators and administrators can see:

  • The hashed IP associated with any post (for ban/spam decisions)
  • Raw IPs stored in the ban table
  • Report content and the hashed IP of the reporter
  • Automod flags on posts (category of flagged content)

Moderators cannot see: your real IP from hashed records, your identity, your other posts (there is no account to link them), or anything beyond what is publicly visible plus the hashed IP.

Data sharing and third parties

@6chan does not share, sell, license, or transfer your data to any third party for any commercial purpose, ever.

The only circumstances under which data may be disclosed:

  • Legal requirement - a valid court order, subpoena, or equivalent legal process from a jurisdiction with authority over @6chan's hosting. In this case we would disclose only what is legally required and nothing more.
  • CSAM reports - child sexual abuse material is reported to the relevant authorities as required by law. The IP associated with such a post (if recoverable from hashed records) may be included.
We will publish a transparency notice on the news page if we receive legal demands for user data, to the extent we are legally permitted to do so.

Anonymity in practice

@6chan cannot identify you by name or account because we have neither. Your post history is not linked - two posts from the same person look identical to two posts from two different people, unless you choose to identify yourself through your writing.

This is not an accident. Anonymity is a feature, not a limitation. It produces more honest discussion, protects vulnerable people speaking on sensitive topics, and prevents the social performance that ruins most social platforms.

The only thread connecting your posts is your IP hash - used solely for moderation, never surfaced publicly, and non-reversible by design.

Your rights

Because @6chan holds no accounts and no personally identifying information beyond hashed IPs, many data rights (access, correction, portability) don't apply in the traditional sense - we cannot locate "your" data without an IP address to search against.

If you have a specific concern - for example, a post containing your real personal information that you want removed - contact us via the contact page. We review these individually and remove doxxing content promptly.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially - for example, if we begin storing additional data or change how data is retained - we will post a notice on the news page with the date and summary of the change. The updated date at the top of this page reflects the last modification.

Questions about this policy can be directed to the contact page.
