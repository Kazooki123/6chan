# Code of Conduct

## Our Goal

6chan is a community project that values respectful collaborations, wholesomeness, and user's freedom of speech.

Everyone is absolutely welcome to contribute regardless of their **identity** AND **experience level**.

## Expected Behavior

Pwease:

- Be respectful toward others
- Give constructive feedback
- Be patient with new contributors
- Keep discussions focused on improving the project
- Assume good intentions if possible

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following is not acceptable within 6chan's development spaces:

- Harassment or personal attacks
- Threats or intimidations
- Spam or intentional disruptive behavior
- Discrimintation against others

## Freedom of Speech

6chan supports freedom of speech, **but project discussions should remain productive and respectful**. Disagreements are normal, but they should never, never ever become personal.

## Enforcement

6chan maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation if behavior repeatedly disrupts the community or violates this **Code of Conduct**.

**Our goal is not punish people, but to maintain a friendly and welcoming environment where every developer can contribute /ᐠ｡w｡ᐟ\\.**
