# GOVERNANCE

This document describes how the **6chan open source project** and the official 6chan website are maintained, administered, and most importantly, **governed**.

Our goal (as the maintainers) is to keep development understandable, moderation accountable, and decision-making transparent without unnecessary bureaucracy.

## I. Two Separate Responsibilities

6chan has two (2) related but distinct areas of governance:

- **Project governance**: The source code, repositories, development decision, releases, documentation, and contributions.
- **Website governance**: The operation of the official website, including moderation, administration, user safety. infrastructure, and enforcement of website rules.

Participation in one area does <span style="color:red;">NOT</span> mean automatically grant authority in the other.

Contributing code does <span style="color:red;">NOT</span> automatically grant administrative privileges on the website. Likeweise, being a website moderator does also not automatically grant write access to the source code.

## II. Project Maintainers

Project maintainers are responsilble for keeping the source code functional, reviewing contributions, managing releases, and making technical decisions.

The project lead or repository owner has the final responsibility for the project unless that authority is explicitly delegated or this governance policy is formally changed.

Maintainers may:

- Review, approve, request changes to, or reject contributions.
- Maintain project documentation and development guidelines.
- Fix bugs and address security vulnerabilities.
- Decide whether proposed features fit 6chan's goals.
- Manage releases, repository permissions, and development infrastructure.
- Appoint additional maintainers when appropriate.

Maintainers should explain significant decisions when reasonably possible, especially when those decisions affect contributors or 6chan's long-term direction.

Maintainer access is a responsibility, not a reward or a measure of someone's **personal worth**.

## III. Contributions and Community Participation

Contributions from the **public** are welcome! Subject to 6chan's applicable [license](LICENSE) and [contribution](CONTRIBUTING.md) requirements.

Contributors may submit bug reports, documentation improvements, feature propossals, code changes, and other useful work.

Submitting a contribution does again not guarantee its acceptance. Decisions may consider correctness, [security](SECURITY.md), maintainability, compatibility, project scope, and available maintaince capacity.

Contributors are expected to communicate respectfully and avoid malicious code, deliberate vulnerabilities, spam, and other harmful submissions.

Anyone may maintain an independent fork of **6chan** under the terms of the applicable license. A fork is automatically an official instance of 6chan and does not automatically represent the official project's decisions.

## IV. Website Administration

The official 6chan website is governed by its designated administrators.

Administrators are responsible for the website's **operation, configuration, security, infrastructure, and administrative permissions**.

Depending on their assigned responsibilities, administrators may:

- Configure and maintain boards and website features.
- Manage moderator permissions and staff access.
- Investigate security incidents and service abuse.
- Perform backups, maintenance, and recovery.
- Enforce website policies and respond to serious incidents.
- Temporarily restrict features or access when necessary to protect the service.

**Administrative access** should be limited to people who need it. Sensitive actions must be protected by appropriate authentication and authorization checks.

## V. Moderators

Moderators help maintain individual boards or the website as a whole, according to the permissions granted to them.


Their responsibilties may include:

- Reviewing user reports.
- Enforcing the published website rules.
- Removing forbidden/illegal content where authorized.
- Issuing warnings, termporary restrictions, or perma-ban where appropriate.
- Escalating serious incidents to administrators.
- Helping resolve disputes about rule enforcement.

Moderators are expected to apply the rules consistently and distinguish between content they personally dislike and content that **actually violates the policy**.

Moderator permissions should be limited to the duties assigned to them. Moderators DO NOT automatically receive unrestricted access to *user data, server infrastructure, source code, or other administrative functions*.

## VI. Moderation Principles

6chan aims to support **open discussion** and anonymous participation while maintaining reasonable boundaries against concrete harm.

Moderation should always focus on conduct and policy violation rather than political affiliation, personal disagreements, or unpopular opinions alone.

The applicable website rules determine what content is permitted. Depending on those rules and the circumstances, moderators may need to address **doxxing, credible or serious threats, coordinated harassment, illegal materials, spamming, and other prohibited activity.

Moderation decisions should be **proportionate** to the violation. When practical, users should receive a reason for significant enforcement actions and have a reasonable way to **request a review**.

No moderation system is perfect. Administrators may review moderator decisions, correct mistakes, and remove staff permissions when necessary.

## VII. Security and Privacy

People with **administrative or maintenance access** are expected to handle credentials, infrastructure, and private information responsibly.

In particular:

- Private user information MUST NOT be disclosed without proper authorization or a legitimate basis. 
- Administrative credentials and secret (keys) MUST NOT be committed to public repositories (this is common sense)
- Security vulnerabilities SHOULD BE handled responsibly, with access restricted to people who need the information.
- Administrative actions SHOULD BE protected against unauthorized access and, where appropriate, recoded for auditing
- Data collection and retention SHOULD BE limited to legitimate operational, security, and moderation needs.

Access to **private information** is a responsibility and must not be used for harrassment, retaliation, or personal gain.

## VIII. Disagreements and Appeals

Disagreements about *code, project direction, or website moderation* should be handled through the appropriate channel or mail.

- Code and development disputes: Discuss the issue through the relevant repository issue, pull request, or project discussion.
- Moderation disputes: Use the website's designated reporting or appeal process, where available.
- Security concerns: <span style="color:red;">Report</span> them privately to an appropriate maintainer or admin rather than publicly exposing exploitable details.

Decisions should be evaluated on their merits, available evidence, security implications, and the project's stated principles.

Project maintainers retain **authority** over the official source repository, while website administrators retain authority over the operation of the official website, subject to applicable law and published policies.

## IX. Changes to Governance

**This document** may change as 6chan grows, gains additional maintainers, or develops a new responsibility.

Significant changes should be documented in the repository so contributors can understand how and why governance has changed.

The project lead or repository owner is responsible for approving changes to this document unless a future governance arrangment explicitly establishes a different process.

## X. Scope and Limitations

**This document** describes the project's intended governance practices. It does not replace the software license, website rules, privacy policy, terms of service, applicable laws, or other authoritative policies.

Where these documents address diffierent subjects, each applies to its **respective scope**.

Governance may evolve over time, but the underlying goal remains the same: to maintain 6chan responsibly, protect the integrity of the project, and support open discussion without *unnecessary* bureaucracy.
